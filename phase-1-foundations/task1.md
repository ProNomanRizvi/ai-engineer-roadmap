## Practical Difference Between an AI Engineer and an ML Engineer

An ML Engineer focuses on building and training machine learning models. An AI Engineer uses those models with other technologies to create a complete AI application.

**Real-world example:**  
A hospital wants an AI assistant for doctors.
- The **ML Engineer** builds a model that can detect diseases from X-ray images.
- The **AI Engineer** creates the full hospital system where doctors upload X-rays, get predictions, view reports, and interact with the AI assistant.
